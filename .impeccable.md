## Design Context

### Users
Mixed audience — parents exploring STEM programs for their kids, potential donors and sponsors evaluating the organization, middle/high school students looking to join or volunteer, and grant reviewers/institutional partners assessing credibility. Everyone needs to quickly understand what Seed does and take action (donate, join, sponsor, or learn more). Desktop and mobile.

### Brand Personality
**Grassroots, smart, welcoming.** A student-led organization that's approachable yet credible. The tone is direct and purposeful — not corporate, not amateur. Reference: nature.org — clean, trustworthy, professional without feeling institutional.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Light (default). The current light background with blue brand accents is appropriate for a nonprofit browsed during daytime by parents, donors, and partners.
- **Palette**: Established blue brand (#7ec5ee → #16587f). Tint neutrals warm, not gray. The blue should feel inviting and energetic, not cold or clinical.
- **Typography**: Keep Inter + Georgia. Tune weights, spacing, and hierarchy. No font churn.
- **Anti-references**: No AI slop patterns (gradient text, glassmorphism, side-stripe borders, sparklines). No over-polished corporate feel. No dark mode with glowing accents.

### Design Principles
1. **Trust through clarity** — Clean layouts, consistent patterns, no gimmicks. Every element earns its place.
2. **Welcoming warmth** — The blue palette should feel inviting. Tinted neutrals, warm undertones. No cold grays.
3. **Grassroots energy** — Student-led isn't amateur; it's fresh, direct, and purposeful. Avoid over-polished corporate aesthetics.
4. **Action-oriented** — Every page should make the next step clear: donate, join, learn, or contact.
5. **Accessible to all** — Mixed audience means clear copy, good contrast, keyboard navigation, reduced motion support.
